My story

My name is Dalton Opudo, and I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Texas at San Antonio (UTSA), concentrating in cybersecurity. I'm originally from Tanzania, and I'm passionate about using technology to solve problems and build applications that make a difference.

Through coursework and personal projects, I've gained experience with software development, database systems, and cybersecurity. One of my projects is SEAConnect, a web application designed to help connect Students of East Africa through events, announcements, meetings, and community features.

I'm always looking for opportunities to learn new technologies, improve my programming skills, and work with others on meaningful projects. My goal is to continue growing as a developer while building secure, reliable, and useful software.
